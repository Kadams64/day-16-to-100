My top three movies - according to Kelly

Not really my top three: just three that came to mind.

Star Wars: A New Hope

This was a movie that changed the way I think about cinema. I watched it at least nine times in the theatre and countless other times at home.

Star Trek: Wrath of Khan

The true return of the Star Trek 'family'. It felt like a classic episode of the original series just stretched out to a feature length movie. The story was great, the acting was great, and the special effects were great. I loved it.

Saving Private Ryan

A powerful war film that follows a group of soldiers on a mission to save a paratrooper behind enemy lines. The movie is known for its realistic portrayal of combat and its emotional impact.
